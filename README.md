# Interview Prep Pack

A prompt-driven system for preparing for senior and leadership job interviews with an
AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.). Research the role and your interviewers,
build and tighten your answers, run realistic mock interviews, and walk in with clean,
glanceable notes.

## What's inside

- **`guide.md`** — the step-by-step prompt guide: the inputs to gather, then the prompts
  to run in order. Works in any AI.
- **`claude-skill/SKILL.md`** — a Claude skill that turns the workflow into a coach you
  can invoke by name. (`interview-prep-coach.skill` is the one-click installable version.)
- **`chatgpt-gpt-instructions.md`** — a ready-to-paste instructions block for a ChatGPT
  custom GPT.

Pick whichever fits your tools. They all do the same thing.

## Who it's for

Anyone preparing for a senior or leadership interview, especially multi-round processes
with panels, performance tasks, or executive finals. It assumes you'll do the thinking.
The AI is a research assistant, a sparring partner, and an honest editor, not a script
generator.

## How to use it

**The guide (any AI):** open `guide.md`, gather the inputs it lists, run the prompts in order.

**The Claude skill:** install `interview-prep-coach.skill` in Claude and say
"help me prep for an interview."

**The ChatGPT GPT:** create a custom GPT, paste `chatgpt-gpt-instructions.md` into the
Instructions field, add the conversation starters, and share the GPT link.

## What you'll need (inputs)

- The full job description
- Your resume / work history
- The company's public info (careers page, mission, recent news)
- Your interviewers' names and titles
- The performance task, if there is one
- Any insider intel from your network
- Notes or a transcript from earlier rounds

Quality in, quality out.

## The workflow at a glance

1. Decode the role
2. Research the interviewers and the org's authentic voice
3. Map the room (who tests what)
4. Build the performance task (if any)
5. Surface and draft your answers, in your voice
6. Mock interview and get grilled
7. Build two reference docs (a clean live glance-sheet + a pre-read)
8. Debrief after each round

Full prompts are in `guide.md`.

## Principles that make it work

- Feed it real inputs, and make it pull your real stories out of you instead of writing generic ones.
- Tell it to be an honest critic, not a cheerleader. Find your gaps before the interviewers do.
- Research your interviewers' authentic voice (interviews, podcasts, their comments on
  others' posts), not just their bio.
- Do the mock out loud, and have it score your concision, not just your content.
- Keep two separate docs: a clean glance-sheet for during, a mindset primer for right before.
- Debrief after every round. Record where appropriate, or brain-dump immediately after, so
  you have game tape.
